- Kharch: gaadi kharidi ki dalali (naya), Hydra charge, Maal bharne ki majuri, Khana kharcha; Chapani hataya
- Har kharch: Rokad / Udhaar, aur Baaki / Chukaya
- Engine + Gear ek item, Dhari + Differential ek item
- Seller: "Vechan / Sell item" – lene wale ka naam, mobile, Rokad/Udhaar, muddat (din); haraji mein jamindar aur dukan number; 💬 se WhatsApp message
- Total mein: kharch dena baaki, udhaar bikri (lena baaki), rokad bikri
